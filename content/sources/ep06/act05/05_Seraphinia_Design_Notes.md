# Seraphinia Character Design Notes

## Core visual
Seraphinia is a young silver-haired woman. She should look youthful but not childish, publicly gentle and approachable yet privately composed and hard to underestimate.

## Clothing
- Inner layer: dark clergy garment (deep grey, indigo-grey, dark slate, or dark muted violet family)
- Outer layer: light ivory / pale cream / soft gold-white ceremonial robe
- Small gold accents allowed at trim, clasp, or holy emblem
- No heavy crown; use refined saintly adornment rather than monarchic regalia

## Acting notes
- Public scenes: warm smile, serene eyes, subtle blessing gestures
- Private scenes: same overall gentleness remains, but eye contact is sharper and timing more exact
- She should feel like a natural political mind who does not need to raise her voice to lead a room

## Story role
She is not an antagonist. Her difficulty comes from intelligence, poise, and institutional skill, not cruelty for its own sake.
