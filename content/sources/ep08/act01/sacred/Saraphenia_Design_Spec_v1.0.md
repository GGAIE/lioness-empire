# Saraphenia｜character art design specification

Status: **Visual working reference v1.0 for this production package**, derived from user-provided image of the white-robed priestess. A package version label is not evidence of pre-existing Canon approval.

- **Reference:** `Saraphenia_Source_Reference.jpg` — user image, center white-clad clergywoman only; seated women are NOT Saraphenia.
- **Turnaround:** `Saraphenia_Ceremonial_Three_View_v1.0.png` — newly generated full-body front, right-facing profile, rear.
- Mature adult, light warm silver-blonde hair in crown braids and long soft waves, calm eyes, fine features, graceful but politically shrewd bearing.
- Outer ceremonial layer: ivory white textile, ornate antique gold embroidered vine borders, wide flowing sleeves and shoulder mantle, removable.
- Inner layer: modest high-neck deep navy garment embroidered with antique gold, retains religious medallion, always fully dressed when mantle is removed.
- Gold dangling earrings and sunburst-style chest medallion; no crown or royal insignia.
- Performance states: public/ceremonial (white outer mantle), private/negotiation (outer mantle laid on chair; navy inner outfit), return to ceremony (mantle back on).
- Keep facial proportions, braid and waves, earrings, clothing geometry consistent through all scene CGs.
- The supplied reference includes a coronation tableau; **do not reproduce its crown placement or imply Saraphenia is the crowned subject.**
- Three-view sheet is a visual identity reference, not a final transparent sprite or proof of all costume material details.
