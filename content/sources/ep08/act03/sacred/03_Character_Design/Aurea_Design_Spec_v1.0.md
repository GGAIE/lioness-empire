# Aurea｜character art design specification

Status: **Visual working reference v1.0 for ACT03 production package**. This is a newly created design for EP08 Sacred ACT03 and is not evidence of pre-existing canon approval beyond the current planning stage.

## Core role
- Name: **Aurea / 奧瑞亞**
- Role: a young tribal girl from the people who have traditionally served the Lava Cat guardian in Valken territory.
- Narrative function: final inheritor of the old covenant, but **not** a chosen-princess archetype. She should feel like an ordinary mountain girl pushed toward a grave responsibility.

## Visual identity
- Age impression: late-teen girl / young maiden.
- Skin: warm tan.
- Eyes: amber-gold, earnest and observant.
- Hair: long dark brown hair with ember-red gradients toward the ends, partially braided, with a few small copper rings and cord ties.
- Expression: gentle, serious, not flashy or coquettish.
- Bearing: modest, village-raised, quietly determined.

## Costume language
- Culture: volcanic mountain tribe that venerates the Lava Cat.
- Palette: cream, charcoal black, rust red, ash gray, copper.
- Garments: layered practical tribal clothing with a ceremonial dimension. Cream inner dress/tunic, darker layered skirts or wraps, asymmetrical shawl or mantle, woven cords and belts, small pouches.
- Decorative motifs: lava-crack patterns, ash/mountain symbols, basalt-stone beads, copper ornaments, claw/fang pendant.
- Footwear: practical strapped sandals or boots with wraps.
- Keep the costume grounded; she should not look like a noble princess, saintess, or academy mage.

## Story-state note
- In early event CGs she may appear in the same base outfit, perhaps more dusted with ash or travel wear.
- In covenant scenes the same design can be slightly formalized with extra cords, beads, or a freshly arranged mantle, but the base identity should remain consistent.
