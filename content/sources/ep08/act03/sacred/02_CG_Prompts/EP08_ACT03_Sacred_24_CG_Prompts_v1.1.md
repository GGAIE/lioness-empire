## Visual continuity checklist
- Aurea’s face, hair, and outfit should stay close to the included three-view reference.
- Alinda should look like a serious territorial lord, not a saintess or court lady.
- The Holy Light side should appear sincere yet institutionally intrusive, not obviously evil.
- The Lava Cat must feel ancient and sacred, with anger rooted in abandonment rather than random bloodlust.
- Do not depict the church literally brainwashing people; “guidance” and “interference” are political and administrative themes.
- The ending must not depict total harmony if the script branch is a failure; CGs should remain broadly reusable, focusing on fixed plot events rather than branch-specific dialogue outcomes.
